# Meta Platforms, Inc. (NASDAQ: META) — Investment Memo

**By Archit Chawla — October 19, 2026** | Price: $709.33 (Oct 16 close) | **Recommendation: PASS**

## Business snapshot

Meta is the best advertising business on earth attached to the most expensive science project in tech. FY2025 revenue was $201.0B (+22.1% YoY): Family of Apps did $198.8B of revenue at a 51.6% operating margin ($102.5B), serving 3.58 billion daily users with ad prices +9% and impressions +12%; Reality Labs did $2.2B of revenue and a –$19.2B operating loss. Management guided 2026 capex to $115–135B and expects Reality Labs losses "similar" to 2025 before peaking. Q3 2026 earnings land October 21 — two days from now; this model is pre-earnings.

## Investment thesis

- **The ad monopoly is genuinely elite.** A 52%-margin Family of Apps growing 22% at this scale, with pricing power (ad price +9%) — there is no better operating leverage in large-cap tech.
- **But the quote assigns zero cost to the AI bet.** $115–135B of 2026 capex plus a ~$19B annual Reality Labs burn, discounted at my 10.1% WACC, is a ~$300/share drag versus the ad business valued on its own. The market is underwriting the entire AI program at a 0% required return.
- **The comps tension is the most interesting thing in this sector.** Median peer forward P/E implies ~$829 — above the $709 quote; the multiple lens says the ad franchise is fairly priced, even cheap. My DCF says $409.83. Both are "right": the market prices the ad business correctly and refuses to charge it for the AI capex cycle. I side with the cash.
- **Reported earnings are lumpy — anchor on cash, not GAAP.** The 2025 OBBBA tax bill triggered a $14.0B valuation-allowance charge that spiked the effective tax rate to 30%; net income actually fell 3% while the business grew 22%.
- **Even my bull case ($652.74) trails the quote.** It assumes Reality Labs losses peak in 2026 as guided and capex normalizes — the optimistic version of management's own story — and still can't reach $709.

The price implies ~6.4% perpetual FCF growth — the lowest hurdle of the four, and still well above nominal GDP on a business about to spend >50% of revenue on capex.

## Key risks

- **The EU ad ruling bites.** The Commission's April 2025 decision against the "subscription for no ads" model could force less-personalized ads in Meta's highest-ARPP region — a permanent margin impairment.
- **Reality Labs losses don't peak in 2026**, and the $115–135B capex ceiling exceeds internal cash generation — buybacks or the dividend get cut first.
- **Ad-cycle downturn.** 98% of revenue is advertising; a recession reverses the +9% ad-price compounding fast.
- **FTC appeal.** The antitrust loss is being appealed with Instagram/WhatsApp separation still a viable legal path.

## Recommendation: PASS

I want to own the ad business and I'm being asked to pay for it plus fund the AI bet for free. At $709.33 there is no margin of safety for the $115–135B capex program — I'd rather own it 30%+ lower or after the capex cycle proves its returns.
