# BlackRock, Inc. (NYSE: BLK) — Investment Memo

**By Archit Chawla — October 19, 2026** | Price: $1,134.38 (Oct 16 close) | **Recommendation: PASS**

## Business snapshot

BlackRock is the world's largest asset manager, monetizing scale in ETFs (iShares), institutional mandates, and the Aladdin technology platform. FY2025 revenue was $24.2B (+18.7% YoY) with $5.6B of net income, a 22.9% net margin — elite for an asset manager, though FY2025 net income dipped from $6.4B in 2024. Q3 2026 results were due October 14 (analysts expected ~$14.36 EPS); the street looks for ~$56 EPS for FY2026 and ~$65 for FY2027. The balance sheet carries net cash (~$10.7B per TipRanks) and essentially no leverage — D/E of 0.33.

## Investment thesis

- **The scale moat is genuine.** No competitor matches the ETF distribution flywheel plus Aladdin; FY2025's 18.7% revenue growth shows the compounding still works.
- **But the price demands a growth miracle.** At 25.8x trailing earnings, the market prices ~7.6% perpetual growth on my payout math — for an asset manager whose revenue is a fixed fee on market levels.
- **The math is not close.** My DCF base ($575.01) sits 49% below the quote even with a 9.4% five-year earnings CAGR and 90% payout; the bull case ($896.74) still trails by 21%.
- **Every lens agrees.** Median peer forward P/E (13.2x) implies $861 and peer P/B (2.57x) implies $954 — both far below $1,134.38. There is no relative-value refuge here.
- **Fee compression is the slow bleed the multiple ignores.** Passive fee pressure and active-to-passive migration cap the long-run growth the price assumes.

## Key risks

- **Market-level leverage.** Revenue is a fee on AUM; a sustained equity drawdown cuts revenue with almost no offsetting cost flex.
- **The multiple is the risk.** At 25.8x trailing, any disappointment in flows or fee realization reprices the stock violently (beta 1.20 cuts both ways).
- **Concentration in passive.** The iShares engine is magnificent until fee compression or a competitor's pricing move compresses the take rate.

## Recommendation: PASS

The best asset-management franchise in the world at a price that requires it to grow like a software company forever — every valuation lens I built sits 16–49% below $1,134.38, so there is no margin of safety at today's quote.

## Methodology & verification notes

BLK is fee-based with no balance-sheet leverage, so I valued equity directly on distributable cash flow (dividends + buybacks from payout ratios on forecast net income) at cost of equity — the standard dividend-discount/FCFE approach, equivalent to an operating DCF at cost of equity, documented on the Assumptions sheet. I caught and resolved the following: (1) Yahoo Finance's beta of 1.421 is an outlier against the cluster (wallstreetnumbers 1.20, TipRanks 1.19, Public.com 1.23, TradingView 1.12) — I used 1.20 and documented the exclusion; (2) the cash-flow statement's "Net Income From Continuing Operations" ($5,942M) exceeds the income statement's Net Income ($5,553M) by $389M in FY2025 — noncontrolling interests of consolidated funds/VIEs; the model anchors on Net Income Common Stockholders, which is unaffected; (3) BLK has no preferred stock outstanding, so the distribution bridge needs no preferred adjustment ($5,298M, 95.4% payout); (4) BLK's 2025 net income dipped YoY ($5,553M vs $6,369M) — the base case grows through it rather than rebasing down; (5) the 10Y yield is already verified at 5.09% (Reuters, Oct 19) and cited as 5.1%; (6) Oct 16 closes were pinned from daily price history, not the live Oct 19 quote.
