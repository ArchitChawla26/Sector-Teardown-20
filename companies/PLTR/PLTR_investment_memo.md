# Palantir Technologies Inc. (NASDAQ: PLTR) — Investment Memo

**By Archit Chawla — October 19, 2026** | Price: $186.03 (Oct 16 close) | **Recommendation: PASS**

## Business snapshot

Palantir builds the operating systems for large-scale data decisions: Gotham for government and defense, Foundry and the Artificial Intelligence Platform (AIP) for commercial customers. FY2025 revenue was $4.48B (+56.2% YoY), and growth is accelerating — Q2 2026 revenue was up 92.8% YoY. FY2025 net income was $1.63B (36.3% margin, flattered by a ~1.4% effective tax rate) with $2.10B of free cash flow, a 46.9% margin. The balance sheet holds $1.8B of net cash.

## Investment thesis

- **The business is genuinely exceptional.** 92.8% YoY quarterly growth paired with 47% FCF margins is a combination almost nothing else in software has ever printed — AIP is a real commercial engine, not a story.
- **But $186.03 prices a miracle, not a business.** 160x trailing earnings and 69x sales embed a decade of flawless execution with zero missteps.
- **My base case is already aggressive — and still says no.** I model 65% growth tapering to 18%, revenue reaching $20.7B, EBITDA margins to 47% — and the DCF lands at $31.79, 82.9% below the quote.
- **Even the bull case needs years of multiple compression.** At $50.69, the bull case requires the stock to go nowhere for a very long time while the business grows into it.

Base case: 5-year revenue CAGR of ~35.7% ($4.48B → $20.7B), EBITDA margin expanding 32.2% → 47%, WACC 12.3% (5.1% risk-free + 1.60 beta × 4.5% ERP), 3.0% terminal growth.

## Key risks

- **Government concentration and budget cycles.** Defense and intelligence contracts are lumpy and politically exposed.
- **Valuation compression.** At 160x earnings, any deceleration — or a rotation out of AI momentum — reprices the stock violently (October 16: –7.2%).
- **Insider selling overhang.** Ongoing 10b5-1 sales add supply and weigh on sentiment.
- **Enterprise AI competition.** Hyperscalers and startups are all selling into the same AIP-shaped opportunity.

## Recommendation: PASS

I admire the business enormously and would not short it — but at $186.03 there is no price at which my DCF gets comfortable, so there is no buy.

## Methodology & verification notes

Every figure in the model was verified against primary sources, and I caught and resolved the following: (1) the FY2021 column in the Yahoo annual pull was blank — excluded, so historicals run FY2022–FY2025; (2) FY2025's tax provision was only $22.7M (~1.4% effective) on $1,657M of pretax income — NOL/valuation-allowance dynamics — so I normalized to the 21% statutory rate forward rather than annualizing a near-zero rate; (3) beta 1.60 used, cross-checked across three sources (range 1.60–1.62); (4) net cash of $1,819M from the Q2 2026 balance sheet (2026-06-30); (5) net-income tie-out across statements shows a max $9.6M diff (minority interest/rounding) — noted, not material; (6) SBC of $684M (15.3% of revenue) is added back in the FCF bridge and disclosed.
