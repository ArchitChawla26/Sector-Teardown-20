# Snowflake Inc. (NYSE: SNOW) — Investment Memo

**By Archit Chawla — October 19, 2026** | Price: $306.84 (Oct 16 close) | **Recommendation: PASS**

## Business snapshot

Snowflake's AI Data Cloud is the leading independent platform for data warehousing, engineering and AI workloads, sold on a consumption model. FY2026 (ended Jan 31, 2026) revenue was $4.68B (+29.2% YoY). The company has never been GAAP-profitable — FY2026 net loss was $1.33B — yet generated $1.12B of free cash flow (23.9% margin). The paradox resolves in one line: $1.60B of stock-based compensation, 34.2% of revenue, the heaviest in this peer set.

## Investment thesis

- **The product is elite and consumption growth is real.** 29% growth on a $4.7B base with 67% gross margins is a genuinely rare asset.
- **But the P&L is still an SBC story.** Add back $1.6B of stock comp and the "profitability" is a choice of denominator — shareholders pay for it through dilution (337.5M diluted shares and rising).
- **My DCF is already generous — and still says no.** I model EBITDA marching from –23% to +18% and the FCF margin ramping 16.1% → 37.9%, and the base case lands at $137.47, 55.2% below $306.84.
- **At an 11.0% WACC the math is brutal.** Growth must be flawless for a decade to justify this quote; the October 16 –8.4% drawdown on AI-financing fears showed how fast the multiple can compress.

## Key risks

- **Consumption volatility.** Usage-based revenue flexes down in a downturn faster than seat-based revenue.
- **Hyperscaler competition.** The big clouds sell competing data platforms bundled with compute.
- **SBC dilution.** 34.2% of revenue in stock comp is a permanent transfer from shareholders to employees unless the decline path holds.
- **Multiple compression.** At 20x sales, any growth miss gets punished — as October 16 demonstrated.

## Recommendation: PASS

An outstanding product at a price that assumes the next decade goes perfectly — my DCF cannot get within 55% of the quote even on optimistic assumptions.

## Methodology & verification notes
