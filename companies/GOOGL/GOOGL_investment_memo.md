# Alphabet Inc. (NASDAQ: GOOGL) — Investment Memo

**By Archit Chawla — October 19, 2026** | Price: $340.59 (Oct 16 close) | **Recommendation: PASS**

## Business snapshot

Alphabet is the AI winner the market has already crowned. FY2025 revenue was $402.8B (+15.1% YoY) with $129.0B of operating income: Search & other $224.5B, YouTube ads $40.4B, subscriptions/platforms/devices $48.0B, and Google Cloud $58.7B (+36%) with $13.9B of operating income. Q2 2026 accelerated: revenue $119.8B (+24%), Cloud $24.8B (+82% YoY, now 21% of revenue), Search +17%. The balance sheet holds $129.7B of net cash. But 2026 capex is guided to $175–185B — roughly 40% of TTM revenue. Q3 2026 earnings are due October 28; this model is pre-earnings.

## Investment thesis

- **The business is firing on all cylinders — and the price knows it.** Cloud growing 82%, Search growing 17%, Gemini at 750M monthly users: this is the strongest fundamental momentum of the four Consumer Tech names. There is no "misunderstood" story here.
- **The capex guide consumes the growth it's supposed to fund.** $175–185B of 2026 capex against ~$165B of FY2025 operating cash flow means the AI build absorbs essentially all internally generated cash. The market is capitalizing capex dollars as if they were already returns.
- **The price implies ~7.4% perpetual FCF growth** (my arithmetic) — on a company whose FCF was $73.3B in FY2025 and is about to be cut in half by the capex cycle. Perpetual 7%+ growth is a claim about the next fifty years, not the next five.
- **The trailing P/E (17x) is a trap.** Q2 2026 net income of $112.2B (+298% YoY) included ~$98B of other income — a one-time gain inflating trailing EPS to $19.93. The honest multiple is 22.3x forward, which implies only $366 — barely above the quote.
- **Net cash is real but not a bridge.** $129.7B of net cash is $10.61/share of option value; it does not close a $176 gap to my $164.95 DCF base. Even my bull case ($243.51) sits 29% below $340.59.

## Key risks

- **AI search disruption.** Zero-click answers compress the Search monetization that funds everything — the existential risk no DCF captures well.
- **The capex returns disappoint.** $175–185B/year of data centers earning utility returns instead of cloud returns is the bear case ($87.64).
- **DOJ remedies.** The antitrust overhang (distribution deals, potential structural remedies) can re-price the Search moat overnight.
- **Cloud price war.** AWS and Azure competing on AI-workload pricing compresses Cloud's 23.7% operating margin just as it scales.

## Recommendation: PASS

The best-positioned AI incumbent at a price that assumes the capex cycle is already paying off. Of the four Consumer Tech names this is the closest call — the comps lens nearly justifies the quote — but my cash math says no at $340.59.
