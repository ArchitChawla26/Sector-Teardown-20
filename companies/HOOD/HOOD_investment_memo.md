# Robinhood Markets, Inc. (NASDAQ: HOOD) — Investment Memo

**By Archit Chawla — October 19, 2026** | Price: $98.87 (Oct 16 close) | **Recommendation: PASS**

## Business snapshot

Robinhood is the leading commission-free brokerage for self-directed retail investors in the US, monetizing through transaction-based revenue (options, equities, crypto), net interest income on customer cash and margin balances, and Gold subscriptions. FY2025 revenue was $4.47B (+51.6% YoY) with $1.88B of net income, a 42.0% net margin — elite economics for a brokerage. Q3 2026 earnings are due October 27.

## Investment thesis

- **Best-in-class unit economics.** 48.7% EBITDA margin and 42% net margins on an asset-light, digital-only cost base; FY2022–2025 shows a clean arc from -$1.0B net loss to +$1.9B net income.
- **Multiple compounding growth vectors.** Gold/retirement adoption, crypto trading share, event contracts and prediction markets, and early international expansion each add high-margin revenue streams.
- **A rate beneficiary priced as if rates stay high forever.** Elevated rates inflate net interest income, but the 10Y sits at 5.1% — a historically rich level that flatters every input to this thesis.
- **The price demands perfection.** At ~20x sales and 44x trailing earnings, the market implies ~40% five-year revenue CAGR or a ~10% WACC (aggressive rate-cut pricing). Even my bull case sits ~49% below the quote.
- **Risk/reward is skewed.** I would rather own the business 30–40% lower, or after the 10Y normalizes toward 4%.

## Key risks

- **Rate cuts compress the best revenue line.** Net interest income is a top contributor; Fed easing directly shrinks it while my DCF's discount rate stays punitive — a double hit.
- **Transaction revenue is cyclical.** Options and crypto volumes drive the P&L; a retail-trading slowdown or a crypto winter reverses the 2023–2025 growth story fast (beta 2.35 cuts both ways).
- **Regulatory and competitive overhang.** Payment-for-order-flow scrutiny persists, and Schwab/Fidelity can retaliate on margin rates and sweep yields at any time.

## Recommendation: PASS

A great business at a price that demands perfection — every valuation lens I built sits below $98.87, so there is no margin of safety at today's quote.

## Methodology & verification notes

I ran an LLM-assisted workflow to accelerate this teardown: automated pulls of filings-derived financials and market data, plus drafting help on structure. Every figure in the model was then manually verified, and I caught and resolved the following: (1) Yahoo Finance's `sharesOutstanding` field (790.6M) contradicted its own market cap ÷ price (899.1M) — I used 899.1M, cross-checked against two independent quote sources; (2) beta 2.35 verified across four sources (range 2.35–2.48); (3) the FY2021 column in the annual pull was blank — excluded, model uses FY2022–2025; (4) 2024 carried a –$347M tax benefit, so I used the 21% statutory rate forward rather than a distorted effective rate; (5) IBKR's negative enterprise value was excluded from EV/Revenue comps; (6) EV/Revenue comps imply ~$7/share, which I judged not meaningful — HOOD's rate-driven revenue mix breaks that multiple, so forward P/E is the operative lens.
