# Broadcom Inc. (NASDAQ: AVGO) — Investment Memo

**By Archit Chawla — October 19, 2026** | Price: $328.75 (Oct 16 close) | **Recommendation: PASS**

## Business snapshot

Broadcom is a two-engine compounder: custom AI accelerators (XPUs) and AI networking on the semiconductor side, plus a high-margin infrastructure-software business supercharged by the VMware acquisition. FY2025 revenue was $63.89B (+23.9% YoY) with EBITDA of $34.71B — a 54.3% margin. Q3 FY2026 revenue hit $29.59B, up 85.5% YoY, with a $0.65 quarterly dividend ($2.60 annualized, ~0.8% yield).

## Investment thesis

- **AI networking + a software cash machine.** The XPU/Ethernet franchise rides the same AI capex wave as NVDA, while VMware's subscription conversion throws off software-like margins — the combination is genuinely hard to replicate.
- **Elite capital allocation.** The VMware deal is integrating ahead of the classic Broadcom playbook: buy, cut, convert to subscription, delever. Net debt is down to $35.4B from the acquisition peak.
- **The quote prices the flywheel as derisked.** My bull case — 31% five-year revenue CAGR, margins to 62% — lands at $334.49, essentially the market price. That means fair value requires everything to go right.
- **Base case is 40% light.** At an 11.5% WACC (beta 1.48 × 5.1% risk-free), a still-aggressive 24% revenue CAGR cannot clear $328.75. The market is underwriting the bull case as the base case.
- **Leverage cuts both ways.** $59.4B of debt at a 6.0% through-cycle cost is manageable, but it means the equity has less cushion in a down-cycle year — my bear case has FY2029 at −12% revenue.

## Key risks

- **VMware integration and customer pushback.** The aggressive subscription conversion has already generated enterprise resentment; churn or regulatory scrutiny would hit the highest-margin revenue line.
- **AI capex cyclicality.** XPU/networking orders are lumpy and hyperscaler-concentrated; a digestion year reverses the growth story fast.
- **Leverage in a downturn.** $59.4B of debt against a cyclical revenue base limits flexibility if the bear case materializes.

## Recommendation: PASS

A superbly run compounder, but the market price equals my bull case — there is no margin of safety, only execution perfection. I would need the stock meaningfully lower, or evidence the VMware margin expansion is structural rather than cyclical, to engage.
